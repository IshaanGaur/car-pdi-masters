Inspection Coverage

Designed for buyers who want a clearer picture of a vehicle before making a purchase decision.

Exterior

Body panels, paint, gaps, bumpers, doors, bonnet, boot and visible repair indicators.

Structure Indicators

Accessible aprons, pillars, legs, boot areas and visible structural observations.

Glass & Lights

Windshields, windows, mirrors, headlamps, indicators, tail lamps and warning functions.

Wheels & Tyres

Tread, wear, sidewall condition, rims and spare wheel.

Interior

Seats, belts, dashboard, trims, carpet, controls and visible condition.

Electrical

Windows, locks, horn, lights, infotainment, cameras and sensors.

Engine

Start-up, idle, visible leaks, fluids, battery, belts and hoses.

Transmission

Gear selection, shifting, clutch operation where applicable and abnormal sounds.

Steering & Suspension

Steering response, suspension observations and unusual noises.

Brakes

Pedal, braking response and visible brake component observations.

AC

Cooling, blower speeds, modes, vents and defogging.

Safety & Documents

Warning indicators, VIN/chassis details, keys, toolkit and available documents.

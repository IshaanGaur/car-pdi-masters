New Car PDI in Noida: What to Check Before You Accept Delivery

A new car can look perfect under showroom lights and still have transit marks, paint variation, missing accessories or feature issues. A new car PDI in Noida gives you a structured way to review the vehicle before accepting delivery.

When should you do a new car PDI?

Ideally, arrange the inspection before you complete the final handover and before the vehicle is accepted as delivered, subject to the dealer's access rules. Share the exact showroom or stockyard location and confirm the appointment in advance.

New car PDI checklist

1. Exterior and paint

Review panels for dents, scratches, paint consistency, panel alignment, glass condition, lamps and visible transit damage. Paint thickness measurements can help identify coating variations on applicable panels.

2. Tyres and wheels

Check tyre condition, visible damage, wheel condition and available manufacturing information. Record observations that differ from the expected delivery condition.

3. Interior and features

Check seats, trims, dashboard, seat belts, controls, infotainment, AC, cameras, parking sensors and other features applicable to the vehicle.

4. Electrical and warning indicators

Operate relevant lights, switches and electrical features. Note warning indicators and other observable issues within the inspection scope.

5. VIN and delivery items

Confirm the vehicle identity and review agreed accessories and delivery items. Documentation verification should be completed separately according to the buyer's transaction requirements.

6. Diagnostics

Selected packages and compatible vehicles may include OBD scanning. Diagnostic results are limited to supported systems and cannot guarantee that every fault is detected.

What happens if a problem is found?

Document the finding, photograph relevant evidence and discuss the issue with the dealer before completing the handover. An inspection records observations; it does not guarantee that every hidden or future defect can be identified.

Luxury Car PDI Guide: What Is Checked Before Premium Car Delivery?

Premium vehicles can have complex features and expensive finishes. This guide explains what a luxury-car delivery inspection can cover and what buyers should verify before handover.

What is Luxury Car PDI?

Luxury Car PDI is a structured pre-delivery inspection for premium vehicles. It reviews the vehicle's observable condition, finish, interior, features and supported systems before handover.

Why is luxury car PDI important?

A premium vehicle can contain more expensive exterior finishes, electronics, cameras, sensors and comfort features. Documenting the vehicle before delivery can help identify observable issues before the buyer accepts it.

What does luxury car PDI include?

The inspection can cover paint and panel condition, panel alignment, glass and lamps, tyres and wheels, interior materials, seats, AC, infotainment, cameras, sensors, electrical features, warning indicators and selected diagnostics where supported.

Are luxury-car electronics checked?

Relevant controls and features can be operated within the inspection scope. The inspector documents observable behaviour and warnings; complex software systems can have limitations and should not be represented as fully tested unless they actually are.

Is OBD scanning useful for luxury cars?

Selected OBD scanning can provide additional information on supported systems. Compatibility varies by vehicle and tool, so it is one part of the inspection rather than a guarantee of complete electronic fault detection.

What should I check on a luxury car before delivery?

Pay attention to paint and panel alignment, wheel and tyre condition, glass and lamps, seat and trim condition, infotainment and cameras, electrical features, warning indicators, accessories and vehicle identity. Features specific to the vehicle should be tested against the agreed specification.

What makes a luxury-car PDI different?

The fundamentals remain the same, but premium vehicles can have more expensive finishes and a larger number of electronic, camera, sensor and comfort features. A useful luxury PDI should therefore pay particular attention to:

  • Paint and finish: panel condition, paint variation and visible repair clues.
  • Wheels and tyres: visible damage, condition and specification-related observations.
  • Premium interior: seats, trim, controls, displays and comfort features.
  • Cameras, sensors and electronics: applicable observable functions and warning messages.
  • Diagnostics: selected supported-system scanning where compatible tools are available.
  • Evidence: photographs and clear findings that can be reviewed before handover.

The inspection should still be honest about limitations: a delivery inspection is not a guarantee that every software, electronic or future mechanical issue has been detected.

Luxury-car delivery checks that are easy to overlook

On a premium vehicle, a buyer can spend more time checking features and still miss simple delivery details. A structured handover review should make room for:

  • Finish and trim: inspect paint, panel edges, glass and premium interior surfaces under suitable lighting.
  • Wheels and tyres: look for kerb marks, visible damage and condition issues before the vehicle leaves the delivery location.
  • Comfort features: where fitted, operate powered seats, memory functions, climate controls, sunroof and other agreed convenience features.
  • Infotainment and connectivity: check the visible operation of displays, controls and applicable media or connectivity features.
  • Cameras and sensors: verify applicable parking cameras, sensor feedback and visible warning messages within the inspection scope.
  • Accessories and keys: compare supplied keys, charging equipment where applicable and agreed accessories with the delivery list.

The purpose is not to claim that every software module has been certified. It is to document observable condition and identify items that should be clarified before handover.

What should I ask before booking a luxury-car PDI?

Ask the inspection provider which areas are actually covered, whether supported diagnostics are included, how findings are documented and what limitations apply to the vehicle. For a premium car, the useful question is not simply “How many checks?” but “Which checks will you perform on this exact vehicle, and what evidence will I receive?”

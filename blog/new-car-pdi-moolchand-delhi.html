New Car PDI in Moolchand, Delhi: Delivery-Day Checklist

A practical new-car PDI checklist for buyers in Moolchand, Delhi, covering paint, panels, tyres, features, diagnostics and delivery evidence.

Why do a new car PDI before delivery?

A new vehicle can arrive with transit marks, paint variation, panel alignment issues, missing accessories or feature concerns. A new car PDI in Moolchand gives the buyer a structured review before delivery acceptance, subject to dealer/site access.

New car PDI checklist

1. Body, paint and glass

Review panels, bumpers, doors, gaps, glass, lamps and visible finish. Paint thickness measurements can be taken on applicable panels.

2. Tyres, wheels and safety

Check visible tyre condition, wheel condition and accessible braking, steering and suspension observations within the selected inspection scope.

3. Interior and electrical features

Operate relevant lights, switches, windows, locks, infotainment, AC, cameras and sensors where applicable. Note warning indicators and observable feature issues.

4. Engine bay and diagnostics

Visible engine-bay items, battery and accessible fluids can be reviewed where safe. OBD scanning is available with selected packages and compatible vehicles.

5. Evidence and report

Relevant observations and photographs are organised into a structured inspection report. The report records the inspection scope at that time and does not guarantee discovery of every hidden or future defect.

What should you do if an issue is found?

Document the finding, keep relevant photographs and discuss material concerns with the seller or dealer before completing the transaction or handover. An inspection supports a decision; it is not a substitute for manufacturer warranty or specialist workshop diagnosis.

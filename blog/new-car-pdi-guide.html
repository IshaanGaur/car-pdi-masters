New Car PDI Guide: What Is It, Why Is It Needed and What Does It Include?

A practical answer guide for buyers asking what a New Car PDI checks, when to do it, where to get it and what to do before accepting delivery.

What is New Car PDI?

New Car PDI is a Pre-Delivery Inspection performed before the buyer accepts a new vehicle. It is a structured review of the vehicle's observable condition and relevant delivery items before handover.

Why is New Car PDI important?

A new vehicle can still have transit marks, paint variation, panel issues, feature problems, missing accessories or other observable concerns. Inspecting before handover gives the buyer a chance to document findings and discuss them with the dealer before accepting the car.

What does New Car PDI include?

A professional inspection can cover exterior and paint, panel alignment, glass and lamps, tyres and wheels, interior and controls, AC and infotainment, cameras and parking sensors, warning indicators, VIN and relevant delivery items. Car PDI Masters uses a 300+ checkpoint framework, paint thickness measurement and selected OBD scanning on supported vehicles/packages, with photo-supported reporting.

When should I do a New Car PDI?

Ideally, arrange it before final handover and before accepting delivery, subject to the dealer's access rules. Confirm the showroom or stockyard location and appointment timing in advance.

Can a brand-new car really have defects?

“New” does not mean that every visible component will necessarily be perfect at the moment of delivery. Transit, storage, assembly variation or feature issues can create observations that a careful inspection may document. The inspection is an evidence-gathering process, not a guarantee against every hidden or future defect.

What happens if New Car PDI finds a problem?

Record the finding with photographs where useful, review the report and raise the issue with the dealer before completing handover. The buyer decides how to proceed.

Dealer PDI vs independent New Car PDI — what is the difference?

A dealer can perform its internal delivery checks. An independent inspection provides a separate buyer-focused assessment and a report that the buyer can review. When comparing providers, check their actual scope, tools, evidence and limitations rather than relying only on marketing claims.

What is checked with paint thickness measurement?

A paint thickness measurement can identify coating differences between applicable panels and help the inspector document observations. It is one inspection tool, not a standalone proof of accident history or every paint-related issue.

Is OBD scanning part of New Car PDI?

Selected OBD scanning is available where the vehicle and package support it. Diagnostic coverage depends on the supported systems and tool compatibility, so an OBD scan should not be presented as a guarantee that every electronic or mechanical fault will be detected.

INSPECTION COVERAGE

What the 300+ checkpoint inspection is designed to review

Exterior & interior

BOOKING GUIDE • SOHNA ROAD

How to arrange a car PDI on Sohna Road

Good access planning makes the inspection more useful. Share the actual vehicle location, delivery or purchase timing and the person who can provide access.

At a showroom or dealer-site

Request permission for an independent inspection, keep the vehicle accessible and confirm where it will be parked. Inspection timing depends on the seller's access rules and the appointment slot.

At home, office or stockyard

Share the complete address or map pin, entry instructions and a contact person. Private premises and stockyards require permission, safe access and enough space to inspect the vehicle properly.

Before delivery or purchase

Where possible, schedule before accepting a new car or finalising a used-car purchase. The report records observed findings and evidence; it does not replace manufacturer warranty checks or specialist workshop diagnosis.

Why Get PDI Done Before Registration in Sector 16?

A pre-delivery inspection can help identify observable transit damage, paint or bodywork concerns and electrical warning signs before handover. The inspection scope depends on vehicle access, package and conditions at the time of inspection.

Where possible, schedule the inspection before final delivery acceptance. Share the vehicle location and coordinate dealership access so the inspection can be completed before handover, subject to appointment and site permission.

CAR PDI SERVICES IN DELHI

Car PDI Service in Delhi for New and Used Cars

New car PDI (pre-delivery inspection) is carried out before you accept delivery from a dealership. It helps review the new vehicle's observable condition, paint and body panels, fit and finish, features and delivery-related items. A used-car pre-purchase inspection has a different purpose: it records visible condition and key functional observations to help inform your buying decision.

Car PDI Masters offers new car PDI and used car inspection services in Delhi using a structured 300+ checkpoint framework. Paint thickness gauge measurements are taken on applicable panels. OBD scanning is available with selected packages and compatible vehicles. Findings and relevant photographs are organised into an inspection report. The actual checks depend on the selected service, package, vehicle compatibility and inspection access.

If you are searching for car PDI service in Delhi, choose the inspection type that matches your vehicle and select the locality where the vehicle will be inspected. For new-car delivery checks, see our New Car PDI service. For a pre-owned vehicle, learn more about our Used Car Inspection.

DELHI CAR INSPECTION FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Car PDI in Delhi

Common questions about new car PDI, used car inspection, paint measurements, OBD scanning and reports.

Do you provide new car PDI in Delhi?

Yes. Car PDI Masters offers new car pre-delivery inspection in Delhi. Arrange the inspection before delivery acceptance, subject to dealership access and booking confirmation.

Do you inspect used cars before purchase in Delhi?

Yes. Used car inspection is available in Delhi to review visible vehicle condition and key functional observations before a buying decision. Actual checks depend on the selected service and vehicle accessibility.

Does the inspection include paint thickness gauge measurements?

Car PDI Masters uses a paint thickness gauge to take coating measurements on applicable vehicle panels. Readings help identify paint thickness variations that may need closer inspection and should be interpreted in the context of the vehicle.

Is OBD scanning included in every car inspection package?

No. OBD scanning is available with selected inspection packages and compatible vehicles. Diagnostic information depends on vehicle compatibility, supported electronic systems and the selected package.

Will I receive an inspection report?

Inspection findings, checkpoint results and relevant photographs are organised into a report according to the selected inspection workflow. You can view the sample report on the website.

Which Delhi localities do you cover?

The Delhi location page lists service-area pages for multiple Delhi localities. Share the exact vehicle address and preferred date when booking so availability and assignment can be confirmed.

Can an inspection identify every hidden defect?

No inspection can guarantee identification of every hidden or inaccessible fault. The report records observations and checks performed within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection.

CAR PDI SERVICES IN DELHI

Car PDI Service in Delhi for New and Used Cars

Car PDI (pre-delivery inspection) helps you review a new vehicle's visible condition, fit and finish, functions and delivery-related items before accepting it from the dealership. A used-car pre-purchase inspection has a different purpose: it helps you review the vehicle's visible condition and key functional observations before deciding whether to buy it.

Car PDI Masters offers inspection options for customers arranging a new-car delivery check or a used-car inspection in Delhi. The checks performed depend on the selected service, vehicle accessibility and inspection conditions. Findings are organised into a report so you can review observations before making your decision.

If you are searching for car PDI service in Delhi, choose the inspection type that matches your vehicle and select the locality where the vehicle will be inspected. For new-car delivery checks, see our New Car PDI service. For a pre-owned vehicle, learn more about our Used Car Inspection.

WHAT THE INSPECTION COVERS

What to Expect from a Delhi Car Inspection

Inspection coverage depends on the service booked and which parts of the vehicle are accessible during the visit. Depending on those conditions, checks may include exterior and body condition, paint and panels, glass, tyres and wheels, interior, electrical functions, visible engine-bay items and delivery-related accessories. No inspection can guarantee that every hidden or inaccessible fault will be identified.

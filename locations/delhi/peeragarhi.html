Car PDI and car inspection in Peeragarhi, Delhi

Peeragarhi is in West Delhi where the Outer Ring Road meets Rohtak Road, next to Paschim Vihar, Udyog Nagar and Mianwali Nagar, with Peeragarhi metro station on the Green Line. The junction is one of the busiest in West Delhi, and the area mixes housing, offices and the Udyog Nagar industrial area.

Many people here drive long daily distances on the Outer Ring Road and Rohtak Road, so used cars from this area often have high kilometres. A careful inspection of tyres, brakes, suspension and the clutch helps you know what money you may need to spend soon after buying.

We cover Peeragarhi, Udyog Nagar, Mianwali Nagar, Jwala Puri and nearby Paschim Vihar blocks. Enter the exact address and parking details when you book.

The report records what could be safely seen or tested at the time of the inspection. It does not replace manufacturer warranty, a specialist workshop diagnosis or a statutory test.

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Peeragarhi

Can you inspect at my office in Udyog Nagar?

Yes, if the office parking allows it and there is space to walk around the car and open the doors and bonnet.

How do you judge if a used car has done very high kilometres?

We compare the odometer with wear on the seats, steering, pedals and tyres, and look at service records if shared. If they do not match, we point it out.

Will the report tell me what needs fixing soon?

Yes. The report lists the problems we found with photos, so you can estimate repair costs and negotiate the price.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

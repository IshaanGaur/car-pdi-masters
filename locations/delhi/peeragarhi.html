LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Peeragarhi

Can you inspect at my office in Udyog Nagar?

Yes, if the office parking allows it and there is sufficient space to walk around the car, open doors, and lift the bonnet.

Are your inspection reports completely independent of local dealerships?

Yes. We maintain 100% zero dealer tie-ups or commissions. We work strictly on behalf of the car buyer to report true facts.

How do you judge if a used car has done very high kilometres?

We evaluate odometer readings alongside seat wear, steering texture, pedal wear, tyre DOT dates, and OBD ECU diagnostic data.

Will the report tell me what needs fixing soon?

Yes. The report details detected problems with photo proof, helping you negotiate prices or request repairs before buying.

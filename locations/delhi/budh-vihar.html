LOCAL CAR INSPECTION • BUDH VIHAR

Car PDI and car inspection in Budh Vihar, Delhi

Budh Vihar is a residential area in North-West Delhi, next to the outer sectors of Rohini and close to Rithala. It has Phase 1 and Phase 2 colonies with independent houses and narrow internal roads, and many families here own budget and mid-size cars.

A lot of the cars bought in Budh Vihar are second-hand, often through local dealers in Rohini or from private sellers. A careful check of body repairs, the clutch, suspension and service record before paying can save you from expensive surprises in the first few months.

We cover Budh Vihar Phase 1 and 2 and the nearby Rohini sectors. Enter the exact address and a landmark when you book.

The report records what could be safely seen or tested at the time of the inspection. It does not replace manufacturer warranty, a specialist workshop diagnosis or a statutory test.

What we check closely on cars in Budh Vihar

  • Accident repairs, repaint and replaced panels
  • Clutch, gearbox and suspension where safely testable
  • For CNG cars: visible kit and the fitment papers the seller shows
  • For new cars: full PDI at the dealer before delivery

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Budh Vihar

Is it worth inspecting a cheap used car?

Yes. On a budget car, one hidden problem like a worn clutch or a repaired chassis can cost a large part of the price. The inspection helps you negotiate or walk away.

Can you inspect at a used car dealer in Rohini?

Yes. Enter the dealer address when you book and inspect before paying the full amount.

The car is parked in a narrow lane. What should I do?

We need room to walk around the car and open the doors and bonnet. If the lane is too tight, ask the seller to move the car to an open spot.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

LOCAL CAR INSPECTION • CHANDNI CHOWK

Car PDI and car inspection in Chandni Chowk, Delhi

Chandni Chowk is the historic heart of Old Delhi, running from the Red Fort towards Fatehpuri Masjid, with Chandni Chowk metro station on the Yellow Line. After the redevelopment of the main street, cars are restricted on the central stretch, and the lanes around it are some of the narrowest in the city.

Families and traders living in and around Chandni Chowk usually keep their cars in public parking lots or outside the walled city. For an inspection, we meet the car at a parking lot, the dealer, or another safe open spot, because a proper check needs space to walk around the car and open everything.

We cover Chandni Chowk, Fatehpuri, Nai Sarak, Ballimaran and the parking areas around the walled city. Choose a parking lot or open spot where the car can be safely checked.

The report records what could be safely seen or tested at the time of the inspection. It does not replace manufacturer warranty, a specialist workshop diagnosis or a statutory test.

What we check closely on cars in Chandni Chowk

  • Bumpers, mirrors and door edges, which get scraped in crowded lanes
  • Signs of long periods of standing in parking: battery, tyres and brakes
  • For used cars: repairs, AC and warning lights
  • For new cars: full PDI at the dealer before you take delivery

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Chandni Chowk

Cars can't enter the main Chandni Chowk road. Where can the inspection happen?

At any safe place where the car is parked, such as a public parking lot, your building parking outside the walled city, or the dealer.

My car stands in parking for days without being driven. What should I check?

Cars that stand a lot often have weak batteries, flat-spotted tyres and sticky brakes. We note these in the report.

Can you inspect a new car before delivery for an Old Delhi buyer?

Yes. The PDI is done at the dealer before delivery, so enter the dealer address when you book.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

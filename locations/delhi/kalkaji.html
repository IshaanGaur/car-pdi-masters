Car PDI and car inspection in Kalkaji, Delhi

Kalkaji is a large residential area in South Delhi next to Nehru Place, Govindpuri, Alaknanda and Chittaranjan Park, with the Kalkaji Mandir metro station connecting the Violet and Magenta lines. It has a mix of DDA flats, independent houses and busy market streets, so parking is often shared or on the road.

Many people in Kalkaji work in or around Nehru Place and Okhla, and prefer to get the car checked at their office parking during the day. Cars parked on the street every night here often show small bumper scrapes and mirror damage, which we record clearly for both new and used cars.

We cover Kalkaji blocks, Govindpuri, Alaknanda, Chittaranjan Park and office parking around Nehru Place. Enter the exact place where the car will be parked when you book.

The report records what could be safely seen or tested at the time of the inspection. It does not replace manufacturer warranty, a specialist workshop diagnosis or a statutory test.

What we check closely on cars in Kalkaji

  • Bumper corners, mirrors and door edges, which often get marked in street parking
  • For new cars: paint, panel gaps, glass and all electrical features before delivery
  • For used cars: accident repairs, tyres, AC, warning lights and the visible engine bay
  • Odometer reading and service record checks where available

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Kalkaji

Can the inspection happen at my office parking in Nehru Place?

Yes, if the building allows it and there is space to walk around the car and open the doors, boot and bonnet. Enter the office address and your parking level when you book.

Do you inspect cars at DDA flat parking in Kalkaji?

Yes. Shared parking is fine as long as the car can be safely checked. It helps if the car is parked where there is some open space around it.

Should I get a PDI if the dealer has already done its own PDI?

The dealer's PDI is done by the dealer. Our PDI is an independent check done for you before you sign the delivery papers, with photos of anything we find.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

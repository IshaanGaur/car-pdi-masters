Car PDI and car inspection in Anand Vihar, Delhi

Anand Vihar in East Delhi is a major transport hub, with the Anand Vihar ISBT, Anand Vihar Terminal railway station and an interchange metro station on the Blue and Pink lines. It sits right on the Delhi border opposite Kaushambi in Ghaziabad, next to Karkardooma, Vivek Vihar and the IP Extension societies.

Because of the heavy bus and truck traffic and the dust around the terminals, cars in this area collect a lot of grime on the paint and glass. On a new car we clean small test spots and check the paint closely under light, so a scratch is not mistaken for dust, and on used cars we look for stone chips and scraped bumpers.

We cover Anand Vihar, Jagriti Enclave side, Karkardooma border areas and nearby societies. Enter the exact address and parking spot when you book.

The report records what could be safely seen or tested at the time of the inspection. It does not replace manufacturer warranty, a specialist workshop diagnosis or a statutory test.

What we check closely on cars in Anand Vihar

  • Paint and glass checked under light, beyond the layer of road dust
  • Stone chips on the bonnet and windscreen, and scraped bumpers
  • AC cabin filter area and AC cooling, which suffer in dusty conditions
  • For new cars bought from Delhi or Ghaziabad showrooms: a full PDI before delivery

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Anand Vihar

My car is very dusty. Can you still check the paint properly?

Yes. We wipe small test areas where needed and check the paint under good light. If the car is covered in thick dust, a quick wash before the inspection gives the best result.

I bought my car in Ghaziabad but live in Anand Vihar. Can you inspect it at home?

Yes. If the car is already with you, we can inspect it at your home in Anand Vihar. For a PDI before delivery outside Delhi or Gurugram, please confirm with us on WhatsApp first.

Do you check the windscreen for chips?

Yes. We note chips and cracks on the windscreen and other glass, because small chips can spread and some insurance policies treat glass separately.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Karol Bagh

Should I get the PDI done before or after fitting accessories in Karol Bagh?

Before. A PDI on the car in factory condition clearly identifies original manufacturing defects. Once accessories are fitted, it is hard to isolate whether a fault came from the factory or aftermarket installation.

Are your vehicle inspections completely independent of local dealers and accessory shops?

Yes. We maintain 100% zero dealer or shop tie-ups. We work strictly for the car buyer to report unbiased technical facts.

Can you check if aftermarket fittings on a used car are safe?

We check visible extra wiring, added lights, and aftermarket fittings using diagnostic scanner tools to detect electrical load anomalies, battery drain risks, or ECU warning codes.

Parking is very crowded in Karol Bagh. Where can the inspection happen?

Any safe spot with sufficient space to walk around the car and open the doors and bonnet works, such as building parking, showroom stockyards, or quieter side lanes.

How do I book and pay?

Submit your details in the booking form, pay the ₹299 deposit via Razorpay, and pay the remaining balance before inspection starts.

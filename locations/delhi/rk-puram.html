LOCAL CAR INSPECTION • R.K. PURAM

Car PDI and car inspection in R.K. Puram, Delhi

R.K. Puram is a large planned area in South Delhi, divided into numbered sectors, with a lot of government housing and offices, close to Bhikaji Cama Place, Munirka and Africa Avenue. The R.K. Puram metro station is on the Magenta Line, and the Outer Ring Road runs along the area.

Many residents are government employees who get transferred, so used cars here change hands often when people move to or from other cities. If you are buying a car from someone being transferred, an inspection is useful because the seller may be in a hurry and you may not get much time to check the car yourself.

We cover R.K. Puram Sectors 1 to 13, Munirka, Bhikaji Cama Place and nearby colonies. Government housing colonies may need prior entry permission, so please arrange it when you book.

The report records what could be safely seen or tested at the time of the inspection. It does not replace manufacturer warranty, a specialist workshop diagnosis or a statutory test.

What we check closely on cars in R.K. Puram

  • Body repairs, repaint and replaced panels
  • Tyres, brakes, suspension and AC
  • Other-state registration, papers and service records shown by the seller
  • For new cars: full PDI before delivery

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in R.K. Puram

Can you inspect a car inside a government housing colony in R.K. Puram?

Yes, if entry is allowed. Please arrange permission with the colony security in advance and share the gate details in the booking notes.

The seller is being transferred and wants a quick sale. Can you inspect fast?

Choose the earliest date in the booking form. Once confirmed, our inspector checks the car and you receive the report with photos.

Do you check cars registered in another state?

Yes. We inspect the car as usual and note the registration details shown. Transfer and re-registration rules should be checked with the transport department.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

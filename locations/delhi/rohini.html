LOCAL CAR INSPECTION • ROHINI

Car PDI and car inspection in Rohini, Delhi

Rohini is one of Delhi's largest planned residential sub-cities, spread over many numbered sectors in North-West Delhi, with the Red Line running through it and the Outer Ring Road along its edge. It has DDA flats, group housing societies, plotted houses and many district centres.

Rohini has many showrooms and used car dealers, so it is one of the busiest areas for both new car PDI and used car inspection. You can book for any sector by entering your exact address.

We cover every Rohini sector, Rohini District Centre, Sector 3 to 24 and the newer outer sectors. Enter your sector and house number when you book.

The report records what could be safely seen or tested at the time of the inspection. It does not replace manufacturer warranty, a specialist workshop diagnosis or a statutory test.

What we check closely on cars in Rohini

  • For new cars: paint, panel gaps, glass and every feature
  • VIN month and tyre date codes
  • For used cars from Rohini dealers: repairs, odometer and service history
  • AC, battery and warning lights

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Rohini

My sector isn't listed separately. Can I still book?

Yes. We cover all of Rohini. Enter your full address when you book.

Can you inspect at a used car dealer in Rohini?

Yes. Enter the dealer address when you book.

Will the inspection help me bargain?

Yes. The report shows problems with photos, which you can use to ask for repairs or a lower price.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Prashant Vihar

Can the PDI be done at a showroom in Rohini?

Yes. Enter the showroom address when you book, and schedule the PDI before you sign the delivery papers.

What happens if the PDI finds a problem on my new car?

You get photos of the problem in the report. You can ask the dealer to fix it before delivery or to give you a different car.

Can you inspect at my home in Prashant Vihar?

Yes. If the car is already with you, a home driveway or society parking works well.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

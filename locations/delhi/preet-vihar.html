Car Inspection Services in Preet Vihar, East Delhi

Planning to buy a car in Preet Vihar? An independent vehicle inspection can help you understand its visible condition before making a payment. Car PDI Masters provides new car pre-delivery inspections and used car inspections at the vehicle's location.

We serve customers in Preet Vihar and nearby East Delhi areas including Nirman Vihar, Laxmi Nagar, Jagriti Enclave, Swasthya Vihar, Gagan Vihar and Karkardooma. An inspection can be arranged at a suitable home, office or dealership location, subject to access and safe working space.

For a new car, the inspection focuses on visible paint and body condition, panel alignment, glass, tyres, lights, electrical features and applicable delivery checks. For a used car, we also record visible signs of repair, interior condition, warning indicators, available service records and other observable concerns.

The report documents observations made at the time of inspection, with photographs where applicable. This is a non-invasive inspection and does not replace a specialist workshop diagnosis or guarantee detection of every hidden or intermittent fault.

What We Check During a Car Inspection in Preet Vihar

  • Exterior paint, body panels, dents, scratches and visible repaint signs
  • Panel gaps, doors, bonnet, boot and accessible structural areas
  • Headlights, tail lamps, windows, locks and electrical functions
  • Tyres, wheels, spare wheel and accessible brake components
  • Interior condition, dashboard controls and warning indicators
  • Visible engine-bay components and fluid leaks, where accessible
  • Odometer reading and service records, where available
  • Vehicle documents and keys presented during the inspection

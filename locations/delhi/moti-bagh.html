Customers arranging a car PDI or vehicle inspection in Moti Bagh can provide the actual vehicle location during booking. The vehicle may be available at a dealership, residence, office parking area or another accessible inspection location, subject to operational scheduling.

For a new car delivery, the inspection can focus on visible delivery condition including exterior body panels, paint and finish, panel gaps, glass, lights, tyres, wheels, interior condition, electrical features, warning indicators and other applicable delivery-related observations.

For a used or pre-owned vehicle, the inspection can document visible body and paint condition, tyre and wheel condition, interior features, electrical functions, warning indicators and applicable engine, transmission, steering, suspension, braking and AC observations that can be safely tested.

For a pre-purchase inspection, the purpose is to create a structured condition record before the buyer proceeds with the vehicle. The inspection can document observable and testable conditions, while faults requiring specialist equipment, diagnostic work, dismantling or workshop evaluation may require separate assessment.

Relevant photographs and inspection remarks are recorded according to the selected inspection workflow. This creates an evidence-based record of the vehicle condition observed during the inspection and makes the findings easier to review afterwards.

Car PDI Masters uses a structured inspection framework covering major exterior, body, paint, glass, tyres, wheels, electrical, interior, engine, transmission, steering, suspension, brakes, AC, safety and delivery-related areas.

The final report reflects observations that can be safely observed or tested at the inspection time. It is not a replacement for manufacturer warranty coverage, specialist workshop diagnosis, statutory vehicle testing or dismantling-based fault detection.

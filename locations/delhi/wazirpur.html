What our Wazirpur car inspection can cover

The inspection is organised across major vehicle areas. Actual checks depend on the selected service, vehicle compatibility, access and safe test conditions.

Exterior, body & paint

Panels, bumpers, doors, gaps, glass, lamps and visible finish observations. Paint thickness measurements are taken on applicable panels.

Interior & electrical

Accessible controls, infotainment, AC, windows, locks, lights, cameras, sensors and warning indicators where applicable.

Mechanical & safety

Visible engine-bay items, battery, accessible fluids, tyres, wheels, brakes, steering and suspension observations where safe.

Diagnostics

OBD scanning is available with selected packages and compatible vehicles. Diagnostic results are limited to supported systems.

Delivery checks

New-car delivery items, visible transit damage, accessories and vehicle identity observations can be reviewed within the selected scope.

Photo-supported report

Relevant findings and photographs are organised into a structured inspection report so you can review the vehicle condition more clearly.

What is a new car PDI?

A new car PDI is a pre-delivery inspection carried out before accepting a new vehicle, subject to site access and appointment confirmation.

What is an old car inspection?

An old car inspection is a structured review of observable condition on a pre-owned vehicle before purchase or handover.

Do you check paint thickness?

Yes. Paint thickness measurements are taken on applicable panels and interpreted with the rest of the inspection evidence.

Can you inspect at a showroom?

Car PDI and car inspection in Malviya Nagar, Delhi

Malviya Nagar is a busy residential area in South Delhi between Saket, Shivalik, Sheikh Sarai and Khirki Extension, with Malviya Nagar metro station on the Yellow Line. Its main market and inner blocks are crowded, and most homes rely on street or shared parking.

Families in Malviya Nagar often buy cars from showrooms in Saket, Okhla and Gurugram, and many young professionals buy used cars as their first car. In both cases a check before paying helps, because small dents, scratches and repairs are hard to spot in a crowded parking spot.

We cover Malviya Nagar blocks, Shivalik, Sheikh Sarai, Khirki Extension and Hauz Rani. Enter the exact address when you book and let us know where the car can be parked for the inspection.

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Malviya Nagar

Can you do the PDI at a showroom in Saket or Okhla?

Yes. Enter the showroom address when you book. The PDI should be done before you sign the delivery papers so any problem can be fixed or the car changed.

Can I be present during the inspection?

Yes, you are welcome to be there. Our inspector can show you the main findings on the car.

Do you inspect two-year-old cars that are still under warranty?

Yes. An inspection before the warranty ends can help you list problems to get fixed under warranty at the service centre.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

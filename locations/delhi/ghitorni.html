LOCAL CAR INSPECTION • GHITORNI

Car PDI and car inspection in Ghitorni, Delhi

Ghitorni lies along MG Road (Mehrauli-Gurugram Road) in South Delhi, between Sultanpur and Arjangarh, with Ghitorni metro station on the Yellow Line and the Gurugram border a few minutes away. The area has farmhouses, the older Ghitorni village and newer residential pockets.

Because Ghitorni is so close to Gurugram, many residents buy cars from Gurugram showrooms. We cover both cities, so the PDI can happen at the showroom before delivery. For used cars, we often inspect at farmhouses or homes where there is plenty of space.

We cover Ghitorni village, the farmhouses along MG Road, Sultanpur and Arjangarh side. Share gate instructions for farmhouses in the booking notes.

The report records what could be safely seen or tested at the time of the inspection. It does not replace manufacturer warranty, a specialist workshop diagnosis or a statutory test.

What we check closely on cars in Ghitorni

  • For new cars bought in Gurugram: full PDI before delivery
  • Underbody and suspension on cars used on rough farm roads
  • For used cars: repairs, AC, tyres and warning lights
  • Premium SUV features and paint

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Ghitorni

My car is at a Gurugram showroom. Can you do the PDI there?

Yes. We cover both Delhi and Gurugram. Enter the showroom address when you book.

Can you inspect at a farmhouse?

Yes. Share the address and gate instructions so our inspector can enter easily.

Do you check SUVs used on rough roads?

Yes. We look at the underbody, suspension and tyres where safely possible.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

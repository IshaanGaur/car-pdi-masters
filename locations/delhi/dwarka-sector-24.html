Car PDI and car inspection in Dwarka Sector 24, Delhi

Dwarka Sector 24 is one of the newer southern sectors of Dwarka, close to the Yashobhoomi convention centre in Sector 25, the Dwarka Expressway and the Urban Extension Road (UER-II). It sits between the older Dwarka sectors and the Gurugram border, so many residents here buy their new cars from showrooms in Dwarka, Gurugram or along the expressway.

Because the sector is still developing, cars are often delivered or parked in open society lots and new roads with construction dust nearby. For new cars we pay extra attention to the paint, glass and panel finish, so you can tell the difference between a transit mark and normal dust before you accept the car.

We inspect in Dwarka Sector 24 and nearby Sectors 23, 25 and 26, and at showrooms along the Dwarka Expressway. Enter the exact place where the car will be, such as the dealer delivery bay or your society parking.

The report records what could be safely seen or tested at the time of the inspection. It does not replace manufacturer warranty, a specialist workshop diagnosis or a statutory test.

What we check closely on cars in Dwarka Sector 24

  • Paint and glass under good light, to spot transit scratches and swirl marks hidden by dust
  • Tyre date codes and odometer reading, to confirm the car is fresh stock
  • Every electrical feature: lights, cameras, sensors, infotainment and windows
  • For used cars bought from the Dwarka and Gurugram area: body repairs, flood signs and service history

LOCAL FAQ

Questions about car inspection in Dwarka Sector 24

My new car is being delivered from a Gurugram showroom but I live in Dwarka Sector 24. Can you still do the PDI?

Yes. We cover both Delhi and Gurugram. The PDI should be done where the car is before you sign the delivery papers, so enter the showroom address in the booking form.

Can you inspect a car in a society parking in Dwarka Sector 24?

Yes, if the society allows our inspector in and there is enough space to walk around the car and open the doors and bonnet. Please inform your security desk about the visit.

Do you check if a new car is old stock?

Yes. We record the manufacturing month from the VIN plate and the tyre date codes, and note the odometer reading, so you know how old the car is before you accept it.

Will the inspection find every possible problem?

No inspection can guarantee that every hidden, intermittent or inaccessible fault is found. Some issues need dismantling or specialist workshop tools, and we tell you when that is advisable.

How do I book and pay?

Fill in the booking form with the car details, inspection address, date and time, then pay the booking amount securely through Razorpay. The remaining balance is paid before the inspection starts.

Questions about car inspection in Green Park

Parking is tight on my Green Park street. Can the inspection still be done?

Usually yes. We need sufficient space to walk around the car, open doors, and lift the bonnet. If the lane is too narrow, we can inspect the car in a nearby open area or at the seller's location.

How is the inspection carried out?

The inspector records observable vehicle-condition findings. Any access restrictions, unavailable checks or diagnostic limitations are noted in the inspection scope or report.

I am buying a used luxury car from a private seller near Green Park. Can you check it?

We can review accessible body, paint, interior, tyres and vehicle features. Diagnostic scanning or battery-related checks are included only when supported by the vehicle, compatible equipment and agreed inspection scope.

How quickly do I get the report?

Report preparation and delivery timing is confirmed for the selected inspection. The report includes findings and photographs collected during the agreed checks.

How do I book and pay?

Send your vehicle make, model, location and preferred timing by WhatsApp or call. The team will confirm availability and share the current booking and payment steps.

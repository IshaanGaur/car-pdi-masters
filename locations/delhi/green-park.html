Questions about car inspection in Green Park

Parking is tight on my Green Park street. Can the inspection still be done?

Usually yes. We need sufficient space to walk around the car, open doors, and lift the bonnet. If the lane is too narrow, we can inspect the car in a nearby open area or at the seller's location.

Are your vehicle inspections completely independent of local dealerships?

Yes. We operate with 100% zero dealer tie-ups or commissions. We work strictly for the car buyer to report objective, technical facts.

Yes. We inspect luxury vehicles using digital paint thickness meters, OBD diagnostic scanners for ECU fault codes, battery health testing, tyre tread depth, and complete feature testing.

You receive a structured digital photographic inspection report immediately after the technical checks are complete, allowing you to make confident decisions before paying.

Detailed Used Car Checks

Our inspection covers multiple vehicle systems and records observations with evidence where applicable.

Body & Paint

Panel condition, paint consistency, dents, scratches, gaps, visible repair indicators and corrosion.

Accident Indicators

Visible signs around panels, aprons, pillars, boot floor and other accessible areas.

Tyres & Wheels

Tread, wear pattern, sidewalls, wheel condition, spare tyre and manufacturing information.

Interior & Electrical

Seats, belts, dashboard, windows, locks, lights, infotainment, sensors and cameras.

Engine & Fluids

Visible leaks, fluid levels/condition, battery, belts, hoses, start-up and abnormal noises.

Transmission & Clutch

Gear engagement, shifting behavior, clutch operation and abnormal sounds where testable.

Suspension & Steering

Steering response, visible suspension condition, noises and ride-related observations.

Brakes

Brake pedal feel, braking response, discs/pads where visible and warning indicators.

AC & HVAC

Cooling performance, blower, controls, vents, modes and unusual noise.

Safety

Airbag/ABS/ESC warning indicators and other visible safety-related observations.

Documents & Identification

VIN/chassis information, keys, supplied items and available vehicle documentation.

Report

Clear findings, remarks and evidence to support an informed purchase decision.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is a new car PDI?

A pre-delivery inspection is a structured check of a vehicle before accepting delivery, covering relevant exterior, interior, electrical, tyres and other inspection areas.

What is a used car inspection?

It is a structured assessment of a used vehicle's observable condition and relevant inspection areas before purchase.

How many checkpoints are covered?

Car PDI Masters uses a 300+ checkpoint inspection framework across major vehicle systems and condition areas.

What happens after I pay the booking amount?

The booking is received and remains pending while the team reviews and assigns the inspection. Final confirmation follows the assignment step.

Can I choose my Delhi inspection location?

Yes. The booking form displays locations currently active and enabled for new orders.

When is the balance payment made?

The remaining balance is intended to be completed before the inspection starts.

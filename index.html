Frequently Asked Questions

What is a car PDI?

Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) is a systematic inspection carried out before a new vehicle is accepted and delivered.

Do you provide used car inspection in Delhi?

Yes. Car PDI Masters provides used car inspection and pre-purchase vehicle inspection services in Delhi.

What is included in the inspection?

The inspection framework covers exterior, body, paint, glass, tyres, wheels, electrical systems, interior, engine, transmission, steering, suspension, brakes, AC, safety and available documents. The final report records the actual checks performed.

When should I get a new car PDI?

Ideally, before final delivery acceptance, so visible condition or functional concerns can be identified and discussed with the dealership.

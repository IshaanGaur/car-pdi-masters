INSPECTION COVERAGE

A Thorough Look at the Vehicle

Our inspection framework is designed to cover the major visible and functional areas of a vehicle.

01

Exterior & Body

Panels, paint, gaps, bumpers, bonnet, doors, fenders, pillars and visible structural indicators.

02

Glass & Visibility

Windshield, windows, mirrors, cracks, chips, markings and operation.

03

Tyres & Wheels

Tread, wear, sidewalls, spare wheel, rims and visible wheel hardware.

04

Electrical & Lights

Lights, indicators, horn, windows, locks, sensors, cameras and infotainment.

05

Interior

Seats, belts, dashboard, controls, trims, carpet, boot and interior functions.

06

Engine & Transmission

Fluids, leaks, battery, belts, hoses, start-up, idle and transmission operation.

07

Steering, Suspension & Brakes

Steering, suspension, brake pedal, discs, pads, handbrake and abnormal noises.

08

AC, Safety & Documents

AC performance, warning indicators, VIN/chassis details, keys, toolkit and documents.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a car PDI?

Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) is a systematic inspection carried out before a new vehicle is accepted and delivered.

Do you provide used car inspection in Delhi?

Yes. Car PDI Masters is positioned for used car inspection and pre-purchase vehicle inspection services in Delhi.

When should I get a new car PDI?

Ideally, before final delivery acceptance, so visible condition or functional concerns can be identified and discussed with the dealership.

Can I get a pre-purchase inspection before buying a used car?

Yes. A pre-purchase inspection can help you understand the vehicle condition before making the final purchase decision.

PDI for Old Car: What We Check Before Handover

A PDI for old car is a structured condition check before you accept a used vehicle. Our PDI service for old car reviews the vehicle’s visible condition and documents relevant findings so you can make a more informed handover decision.

A used-car PDI is useful when the vehicle is being presented for handover by a seller or dealership and you want a documented condition check before accepting it. The focus is on what can be observed at that appointment: visible condition, working features within the inspection workflow, items represented as included and any remarks that should be discussed before handover. It does not authenticate seller documents or establish the vehicle’s complete history.

USED CAR PDI FAQS

Common Questions About Used Car PDI

A PDI for old car and a used-car inspection are buyer-focused checks designed to document observable vehicle condition before purchase or handover. The exact scope depends on the agreed inspection workflow and vehicle access.

When should I book a PDI for old car?

A pre delivery inspection for old car can be arranged before you accept the vehicle handover so visible condition and agreed features can be documented.

For a used car, the inspection should ideally be completed before final purchase or handover where possible, so relevant observations can be reviewed before the transaction is completed.

What does an old car PDI check?

The inspection covers relevant visible areas such as body and paint, glass and lights, tyres and wheels, interior controls, electrical features, accessible engine-bay areas, relevant mechanical observations and safety-related items.

What is a pre delivery inspection for old car?

A pre delivery inspection for old car is a structured condition check carried out before a used vehicle is accepted or handed over. It records relevant observable findings so they can be reviewed with the seller before the transaction is completed.

Can an inspection confirm the complete accident or service history?

An inspection records observations from the vehicle and checks included in the defined workflow. It does not by itself guarantee a complete historical record of every accident, repair or service event.

Do you provide a report?

The inspection workflow records checkpoint results, observations and relevant photo evidence in the inspection report.

Does every checkpoint require a photograph?

No. Photo requirements depend on the inspection workflow and the condition or result of the individual checkpoint.

Does the inspection guarantee that a used car has no defects?

No inspection can guarantee that a vehicle has no defects. The report reflects observations and checks performed within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection.

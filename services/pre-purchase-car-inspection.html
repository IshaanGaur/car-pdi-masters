Why a Pre-Purchase Car Inspection Matters

Before buying a used or pre-owned vehicle, buyers can benefit from a structured review of its present observable condition. A pre-purchase inspection helps organise relevant vehicle observations before a purchase decision is finalised. Car PDI Masters follows a defined inspection workflow covering major accessible areas and records checkpoint findings, observations and relevant photographs in the inspection report.

PRE-PURCHASE INSPECTION FAQS

Common Questions Before Buying a Car

When should I get a pre-purchase car inspection?

The inspection should ideally be completed before finalising the purchase or taking handover, so relevant vehicle observations can be reviewed before the transaction is completed.

What does a pre-purchase car inspection check?

The inspection covers relevant visible areas such as body and paint, glass and lights, tyres and wheels, interior controls, electrical features, accessible engine-bay areas, relevant mechanical observations and safety-related items.

Can the inspection confirm the complete accident or service history?

No. The inspection records observations from the vehicle and checks included in the defined workflow. It does not by itself guarantee a complete historical record of every accident, repair or service event.

Will I receive an inspection report?

The inspection workflow records checkpoint results, observations and relevant photo evidence in the inspection report.

Does every checkpoint require a photograph?

No. Photo requirements depend on the inspection workflow and the condition or result of the individual checkpoint.

Does the inspection guarantee that the car is free from defects?

No inspection can guarantee that a vehicle has no defects. The report reflects observations and checks performed within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection.

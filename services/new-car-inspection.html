Inspection for New Car: Why Inspect Before Delivery?

An inspection for new car is a structured check before delivery. Our new car inspection reviews visible condition, fit and finish, features, tyres, interior and delivery items so issues can be documented before handover.

A new vehicle is expected to be delivered in the condition agreed with the customer. A pre-delivery inspection provides an opportunity to review observable condition and relevant features before delivery acceptance. Car PDI Masters follows a structured inspection workflow so visible observations can be documented systematically rather than relying only on a quick delivery-day walk-around. This is an independent car inspection service designed to document observable findings before acceptance.

NEW CAR INSPECTION FAQS

Common Questions About New Car Inspection

When should I book a new car inspection?

For a new car, the inspection should ideally be scheduled before delivery acceptance where possible, so relevant observations can be recorded at the appropriate stage.

What is checked during a new car inspection?

The inspection covers relevant visible areas such as body and paint, fit and finish, glass and lights, tyres and wheels, interior controls, electrical features, accessible engine-bay areas, safety-related observations and delivery items.

Is a new car inspection the same as a PDI?

Both services focus on reviewing a new vehicle before delivery, but the exact inspection scope and workflow depend on the service selected and the applicable checkpoints.

Do you provide a report?

The inspection workflow records checkpoint results, observations and relevant photo evidence in the inspection report.

Does every checkpoint require a photograph?

No. Photo requirements depend on the inspection workflow and the condition or result of the individual checkpoint.

Does an inspection guarantee that a vehicle has no defects?

No inspection can guarantee that a vehicle has no defects. The report reflects observations and checks performed within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection.

360° INSPECTION FAQS

Common Questions

What is a 360° car inspection?

It is a structured vehicle inspection that combines relevant inspection checkpoints with a 360° walk-around workflow to review and document the vehicle's observable condition.

Does 360° mean every part of the vehicle is completely inspected?

No. The 360° feature refers to the walk-around inspection workflow. The complete inspection scope depends on the defined checkpoints and accessible areas of the vehicle.

What areas are covered?

Coverage includes relevant exterior and body areas, paint, glass and lights, tyres and wheels, interior and controls, electrical features, accessible engine-bay areas, relevant mechanical observations, AC and safety-related items.

Do you provide photographs?

Relevant photographs are captured where required by the inspection workflow and checkpoint result. The available 360° walk-around workflow also provides an additional visual record.

Will I receive an inspection report?

The inspection workflow records checkpoint results, observations and available evidence in the inspection report.

Does the inspection guarantee that the vehicle has no defects?

No. The report reflects observations and checks performed within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection and cannot guarantee that every hidden or future defect will be identified.

NEW CAR PDI EXPLAINED

What Is a New Car PDI?

New Car PDI stands for New Car Pre-Delivery Inspection. It is a structured inspection carried out before a buyer accepts delivery of a new vehicle. The purpose is to review the vehicle's observable condition, identify inspection findings and record relevant observations before acceptance.

A PDI service for a new car can include checks of exterior panels, paint appearance, glass, lights, tyres, wheels, interior, controls, electrical features, accessible engine-bay areas and delivery items. The actual checks depend on the inspection workflow and the vehicle's condition at the time of inspection.

NEW CAR PDI CHECKLIST

What Is Checked During a New Car PDI?

A new car PDI checklist helps review the vehicle systematically before delivery. Car PDI Masters follows a structured inspection workflow covering relevant visible vehicle areas, features and delivery items. Checks are subject to accessibility and applicability.

1. Exterior Body and Paint

Review visible body panels, paint appearance, bumpers, doors, bonnet, boot, panel alignment, scratches, dents and other fit-and-finish observations.

2. Glass, Mirrors and Lights

Review visible glass condition, windscreen, windows, mirrors, headlamps, tail lamps, indicators and other accessible lighting components.

3. Tyres and Wheels

Check visible tyre condition, tread and sidewall observations, wheel condition and related accessible items.

4. Interior and Cabin

Review seats, upholstery, dashboard, trims, carpets, roof lining, visible interior condition and cabin fit and finish.

5. Electrical Features and Controls

Review relevant switches, displays, lights, indicators, electrical features and accessible functional checks where applicable.

6. AC and Climate Controls

Review climate control settings and accessible AC functions according to the inspection workflow.

7. Engine Bay and Accessible Areas

Review accessible engine-bay areas and relevant visible observations within the defined inspection scope.

8. Warning Indicators and Safety Observations

Review relevant warning indicators, visible safety-related items and accessible observations where applicable.

9. Documents and Delivery Items

Review relevant documents, keys, accessories, equipment and delivery items where available for inspection.

10. Inspection Findings and Photographs

Record checkpoint results, relevant observations and photographs according to the inspection workflow.

Frequently Asked Questions About New Car PDI

Answers to common questions about new car PDI, pre-delivery inspection, booking and inspection reports.

What is a new car PDI?

New car PDI means new car pre-delivery inspection. It is a structured check carried out before accepting delivery of a new vehicle. It reviews visible condition, body and paint, fit and finish, tyres, interior, electrical features and relevant delivery items.

When should I get a PDI for my new car?

Schedule the PDI before signing final delivery acceptance or taking delivery, where the dealership permits access. This gives you an opportunity to review and record relevant observations before acceptance.

What does a PDI service for a new car include?

It includes relevant checks of exterior panels, paint appearance, glass, lights, tyres, wheels, interior, controls, electrical features, accessible engine-bay areas, warning indicators and delivery items according to the inspection workflow.

Is new car PDI different from new car inspection?

The terms are often used for checks carried out before accepting a new vehicle. PDI specifically means pre-delivery inspection. The actual inspection scope depends on the selected service and its checkpoints.

Does Car PDI Masters provide an inspection report?

The inspection workflow records checkpoint results, observations and relevant photo evidence in a structured inspection report.

Can I get a new car PDI in Delhi?

Can I book a PDI at the dealership?

Does a new car PDI guarantee that the vehicle has no defects?

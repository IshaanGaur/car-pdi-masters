DEALER PDI VS INDEPENDENT PDI

Why Get an Independent New Car PDI If the Dealer Already Does a PDI?

A dealer's PDI is part of the dealership's delivery process. An independent PDI is a separate buyer-arranged inspection that documents the allocated vehicle's observed condition before handover. The two checks can serve different purposes and should not be treated as interchangeable.

Who Arranges the Check?

The dealer performs its own delivery checks. Car PDI Masters is booked separately by the buyer and inspects the specific vehicle allocated for delivery, subject to dealership access.

Independent Evidence

Findings from the independent inspection are recorded in a separate buyer-focused report with relevant photographs and applicable readings, creating a documented record before delivery acceptance.

Paint & Diagnostic Checks

Paint thickness measurement and selected OBD diagnostics can add evidence beyond a basic visual walk-around. Availability depends on the vehicle, package and access conditions.

Discuss Findings Before Handover

If an issue is documented, you can use the inspection findings and photographs when discussing correction, replacement or clarification with the dealership before accepting the vehicle.

Frequently Asked Questions About New Car PDI

Answers to common questions about new car PDI, pre-delivery inspection, booking and inspection reports.

What does a new car PDI check before delivery?

A new car PDI (pre-delivery inspection) checks exterior body panels and paint thickness, VIN and manufacturing details, tyre batch codes, interior electronics and AC performance, engine bay and fluid levels, visible underbody components, and supported OBD diagnostics—all documented with photo evidence.

When is the best time to book a PDI for a new car?

The best time to schedule a PDI for a new car is after the dealership allocates your specific chassis number (VIN) and the car arrives at their stockyard, before final delivery acceptance, registration or final payment where access is permitted.

How long does a new car pre-delivery inspection take?

A thorough 300+ checkpoint inspection typically takes around 45–60 minutes, depending on the vehicle model, stockyard lighting and inspection conditions. Additional investigation can take longer where required.

What if the dealership asks why an independent inspector is visiting?

Most dealerships may cooperate when informed beforehand that a buyer is bringing an independent inspection professional. Access depends on the dealership, location and inspection arrangements.

Do you provide a written report and photos after the inspection?

Yes. Every new car inspection includes a structured digital report containing checkpoint observations, paint thickness readings, diagnostic scan notes where supported, and relevant photo evidence. You can also view a sample PDI report online.

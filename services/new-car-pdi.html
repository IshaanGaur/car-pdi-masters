What Does a New Car PDI Check?

The inspection is designed to identify visible defects, inconsistencies and delivery-related concerns before acceptance.

Exterior & Paint

Panels, paint consistency, scratches, dents, gaps, alignment, bumpers, bonnet, doors and boot.

Glass & Visibility

Windshield, windows, mirrors, wipers and washers, including visible damage or fitment concerns.

Tyres & Wheels

Tyre condition, visible wear, sidewalls, rims, spare wheel and supplied wheel-related items.

Electrical & Lights

Lights, indicators, horn, windows, locks, infotainment, cameras, sensors and dashboard functions.

Interior & Comfort

Seats, trims, dashboard, controls, seat belts, AC, blower, vents and visible interior condition.

Engine & Mechanical

Visible engine-bay condition, fluids, battery, leaks, start-up behavior and other accessible observations.

Transmission & Running

Gear selection and operation where testable, plus steering, suspension and braking observations.

Safety & Warning Signs

Instrument-cluster warnings and other visible safety-related indicators and functions.

Delivery Items

Keys, toolkit, spare wheel, accessories and available delivery documentation.

Why a New Car PDI Matters Before Delivery

Buying a new car is a major decision, and a pre-delivery inspection gives you an opportunity to review the vehicle's observable condition before accepting delivery. Car PDI Masters follows a structured inspection approach covering the exterior, body and paint, interior, electrical features, tyres and wheels, accessible engine-bay areas, safety-related observations, delivery items and relevant photo evidence.

Check the Vehicle Before Acceptance

A new-car PDI is performed before delivery acceptance so visible condition and relevant inspection observations can be recorded at the appropriate stage.

Review Fit, Finish & Condition

Exterior panels, paint appearance, glass, lamps, mirrors, bumpers and other visible areas are included within the inspection scope.

Check Features & Visible Systems

Relevant electrical features, controls, displays, climate functions, tyres, wheels and accessible vehicle areas are reviewed as part of the structured inspection.

Receive Structured Findings

Checkpoint results, observations and relevant photographs are organised into the inspection report so the vehicle condition is easier to review.

NEW CAR PDI FAQS

Common Questions About New Car PDI

When should I book a new car PDI?

For a new car, the inspection should be scheduled before delivery acceptance where possible, so relevant observations can be recorded at the appropriate stage.

What does a new car PDI check?

The inspection covers relevant visible areas such as body and paint, fit and finish, glass and lights, tyres and wheels, interior controls, electrical features, accessible engine-bay areas, safety-related observations and delivery items.

Do you provide a report after the inspection?

The inspection workflow records checkpoint results, observations and relevant photo evidence in the inspection report.

Does every checkpoint require a photograph?

No. Photo requirements depend on the inspection workflow and the condition or result of the individual checkpoint.

Can a new car PDI be done across Delhi?

Service availability depends on the active locations configured for bookings. Explore the Delhi service-area pages for local information.

Does a PDI guarantee that a vehicle has no defects?

No inspection can guarantee that a vehicle has no defects. The report reflects observations and checks performed within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection.

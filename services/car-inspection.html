A structured car inspection helps organise observations about a vehicle's visible condition and applicable features before delivery, purchase or handover. The inspection is designed around defined checkpoints rather than relying only on a quick visual walk-around.

CAR INSPECTION FAQS

Common Questions

What is a car inspection?

A car inspection is a structured review of applicable vehicle condition and functions. The inspection scope depends on the vehicle, accessible areas and defined workflow.

Do you inspect both new and used cars?

Yes. Separate service workflows are available for new vehicles, used vehicles and pre-purchase inspection requirements.

When should I get a used car inspected?

Where possible, arrange the inspection before final purchase or handover so the observations can be considered before completing the transaction.

Can the inspection identify every mechanical problem?

No. A structured inspection should not be treated as a complete mechanical or diagnostic certification. Findings are limited to the defined inspection scope and accessible checks.

Do you provide photographs?

Relevant photographs are captured where required by the inspection workflow. Not every checkpoint necessarily requires a photograph.

Will I receive an inspection report?

Checkpoint results, observations and relevant photo evidence are organised into the inspection report according to the workflow.

Does the inspection guarantee that the car has no defects?

No inspection can guarantee that a vehicle has no defects. The report reflects observations and checks performed within the defined scope at the time of inspection.

Is car inspection available across Delhi?

Service availability depends on the active locations configured for bookings. Visit the Delhi service-area page for location information.

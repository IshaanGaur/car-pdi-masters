What Is a Luxury Car PDI?

A Luxury Car PDI is a pre-delivery inspection for a premium vehicle that reviews its visible condition, premium features, accessible systems and delivery items before handover.

What Premium Features Are Checked?

Depending on the vehicle configuration, the workflow reviews premium paint and panels, seats and trim, infotainment, displays, cameras, sensors, sunroof, comfort controls, AC and other accessible features.

Do You Check Cameras, Sensors and ADAS?

Applicable cameras, sensors, displays and warning indicators may be reviewed where accessible. The inspection is not a certification of every ADAS function.

Where Is Luxury Car PDI Available?

Car PDI Masters currently offers Luxury Car PDI across Delhi NCR, including Delhi, Gurugram and Noida, with appointment coverage subject to the inspection location and access arrangements.

Will I Receive Photos and a Report?

Relevant photographs, checkpoint results and observations are organised into the inspection report according to the inspection workflow.

Does Luxury Car PDI Guarantee a Defect-Free Car?

No. The report reflects the checks and observations performed within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection.

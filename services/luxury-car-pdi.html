LUXURY CAR PDI FAQS

Common Questions

What is a Luxury Car PDI?

It is a pre-delivery inspection for a luxury or premium vehicle that reviews relevant exterior, interior, electronic, comfort, tyre, safety and delivery areas within the defined inspection workflow.

Do you check luxury car electronics?

Applicable infotainment, displays, switches, cameras, sensors and accessible electronic features are reviewed where supported by the vehicle and inspection workflow.

Do you check the sunroof and seat functions?

Applicable sunroof, seat controls and accessible comfort functions can be checked according to the vehicle configuration and inspection workflow.

Do you check ADAS features?

Relevant accessible cameras, sensors, displays and warning indicators may be reviewed where applicable. The inspection should not be treated as a complete certification of every ADAS function.

Will I receive photographs and a report?

Relevant photographs are captured where required by the inspection workflow, and checkpoint results and observations are organised into the inspection report.

Does the Luxury Car PDI guarantee that the vehicle has no defects?

No inspection can guarantee that a vehicle has no defects. The report reflects observations and checks performed within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection.

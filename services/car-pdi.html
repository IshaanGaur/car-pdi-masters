CAR PDI FAQS

Common Questions About Car PDI

What is a car PDI?

A car PDI, or pre-delivery inspection, is a structured review of applicable vehicle condition and functions before delivery or handover. The inspection scope depends on the vehicle and defined workflow.

When should I book a car PDI?

For a new car, the inspection is generally intended to take place before delivery acceptance. For a used car, an inspection can be arranged before final purchase or handover where possible.

Do you inspect both new and used cars?

Yes. Car PDI Masters has separate service workflows for new cars, used cars and pre-purchase inspection requirements.

Do you provide photographs?

Relevant photographs are captured where required by the inspection workflow. Not every checkpoint necessarily requires a photograph.

Do I receive an inspection report?

The inspection workflow records checkpoint results, observations and relevant photo evidence in the inspection report.

Does a car PDI guarantee that the vehicle has no defects?

No inspection can guarantee that a vehicle has no defects. The report reflects observations and checks performed within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection.

Can a PDI confirm complete mechanical condition?

A visual and accessible inspection should not be treated as a complete mechanical or diagnostic certification. Findings are limited to the defined inspection scope and accessible checks.

Is car PDI available across Delhi?

Service availability depends on the active locations configured for bookings. Visit the Delhi service-area page for location information.

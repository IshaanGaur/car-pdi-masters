FAQS

Common questions

When should I book the inspection?

For a new car, book before delivery acceptance. For a used car, book before final purchase or handover where possible.

Do you provide a report?

The inspection workflow records checkpoint results, observations and relevant photo evidence in the inspection report.

Does every checkpoint always require a photo?

No. Photo requirements depend on the inspection workflow and the condition/result of the checkpoint.

Is inspection available across Delhi?

Service availability depends on the active locations configured for bookings. Explore the Delhi service-area pages for local information.

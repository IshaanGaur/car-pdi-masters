What Does a Used Car Inspection Check?

Our inspection records observations across major areas that can matter when evaluating a used vehicle.

Body & Paint

Panels, paint consistency, dents, scratches, gaps, alignment and visible repair indicators.

Accident Indicators

Visible observations around accessible aprons, pillars, legs, boot areas and other structural indicators.

Tyres & Wheels

Tread, wear pattern, sidewalls, rims, spare tyre and visible tyre information.

Interior & Electrical

Seats, belts, dashboard, windows, locks, lights, infotainment, sensors and cameras.

Engine & Fluids

Start-up, idle, visible leaks, fluid observations, battery, belts, hoses and abnormal noises.

Transmission & Clutch

Gear engagement, shifting, clutch operation where applicable and abnormal sounds.

Steering & Suspension

Steering response, visible suspension condition, ride-related observations and unusual noises.

Brakes

Brake pedal feel, braking response, warning indicators and visible brake component observations.

AC & HVAC

Cooling, blower speeds, vents, modes, controls and unusual noise.

Safety Systems

Airbag, ABS, ESC and other visible warning indicators or safety-related observations.

Identification & Documents

VIN/chassis information, keys, supplied items and available vehicle documentation.

Clear Reporting

Findings, remarks and evidence are organized to help you make a more informed buying decision.

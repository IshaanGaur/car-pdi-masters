WHY EV PDI MATTERS

Why Inspect an Electric Car Before Delivery?

An electric vehicle has many of the same exterior, interior, tyre, safety and delivery considerations as a conventional car, along with EV-specific charging-related equipment, displays and controls. A structured PDI helps review these areas before delivery acceptance.

Review Displays & Controls

EV CAR PDI FAQS

Common Questions

What is an EV Car PDI?

EV Car PDI is a pre-delivery inspection for an electric vehicle that reviews relevant exterior, interior, electrical, charging-related, tyre, safety and delivery areas within the defined inspection workflow.

Do you check the EV battery health?

The inspection should not be understood as a complete battery-health certification. The report reflects the checks and observations available within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection.

Do you check charging-related items?

Applicable charging-related equipment and accessible charging items can be reviewed according to the inspection workflow and vehicle configuration.

Do you check the EV warning indicators and displays?

Relevant displays, warning indicators, controls and accessible electrical features are checked where applicable within the inspection workflow.

Will I receive an inspection report?

The inspection workflow records checkpoint results, observations and relevant photo evidence in the inspection report.

Does the EV PDI guarantee that the vehicle has no defects?

No inspection can guarantee that a vehicle has no defects. The report reflects observations and checks performed within the defined inspection scope at the time of inspection.
